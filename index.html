Short videos about science and history

Getu is an independent French channel that creates short explanatory videos on astronomy, physics, history and economics. Each video is written from verified sources and published on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

The Getu publishing tool

To publish the same video on every platform, Getu uses a small in-house publishing tool. It is used only by the Getu team, for Getu's own accounts. For TikTok, it works like this:

  1. The Getu team connects its TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit.
  2. Each upcoming video is reviewed in the tool: the creator sees which TikTok account it will be posted to, watches a preview, edits the title, chooses who can view the video, chooses whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, and declares any commercial content.
  3. Only after this explicit approval is the video posted to TikTok through the Content Posting API, at its scheduled time. Videos that are not approved are sent to the account's TikTok drafts instead.

The tool does not access any other TikTok user's account or data.
