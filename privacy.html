Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what data the Getu publishing tool ("the Tool") handles, why, and how you can control it. The Tool is operated by Getu, an independent content creator based in France, to publish Getu's own videos on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. Contact: citotor31@gmail.com.

1. Who uses the Tool

The Tool is used only by the Getu team to manage Getu's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data about viewers of Getu's videos or about any other TikTok user.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

When the Getu account is connected through TikTok Login Kit, the Tool receives, with the account owner's consent:

  • basic profile information: open ID, display name, username and avatar (scope user.info.basic);
  • an access token and a refresh token, used to call the TikTok API on the account's behalf;
  • the creator's posting settings returned by the Content Posting API (available privacy levels, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are turned off, maximum video duration);
  • the identifiers and status of each upload or post made through the Tool (scopes video.upload and video.publish).

3. How we use it

  • to show which TikTok account a video will be posted to and which options are available;
  • to upload Getu's videos and post them with the title, privacy level, interaction settings and commercial content disclosure chosen by the creator;
  • to check that each post was completed and keep a record of what was published.

We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than publishing Getu's own content. We never sell or rent it.

4. Storage and security

Tokens, posting choices and post records are stored in a private, access-controlled PostgreSQL database used only by the Tool, and in configuration files on the Getu team's own computer. Connections to TikTok use HTTPS. App credentials are never published or shared.

5. Sharing

TikTok data is not shared with third parties. Videos are sent only to the platform the creator chose. Infrastructure providers (database hosting) process data solely on our behalf.

6. Retention

Access tokens expire after 24 hours and are refreshed while the account stays connected. Post records are kept for as long as the Getu account is managed by the Tool, to track what was published. Video files are deleted from the Tool's storage once they have been published.

7. Your choices

  • Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok: Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services. The Tool can then no longer access the account.
  • You can ask us to delete all data the Tool holds by writing to citotor31@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.
  • Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and you may complain to the French data protection authority (CNIL).

8. YouTube and Instagram

The Tool also publishes Getu's videos on YouTube through the YouTube API Services and on Instagram through the Instagram Graph API. Their use is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy. Google access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

9. Changes

We will update this page if the way the Tool handles data changes, and change the date above.
